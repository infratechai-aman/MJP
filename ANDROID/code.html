FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a slot? +
You can book directly using the online slot booking tool above or via the Playo app by choosing your preferred date and time.
What are the opening hours? +
MJP Badminton Court is open every day from 6:00 AM in the morning to 12:00 AM midnight.
What facilities are available? +
We provide two wooden courts, spacious spectator viewing gallery, separate washrooms, and shower rooms.
Is there a dress code or footwear requirement? +
Yes, non-marking badminton shoes are mandatory to safeguard the high quality wooden flooring. Standard sports attire is required.
