## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-modern, high-performance athletic brand identity for a premier indoor sports facility. The visual language blends elite sports club prestige with modern technological precision.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Disciplined Precision:** Architectural structure, sharp alignments, and confident metric hierarchy communicate tournament-grade standards and pristine facility upkeep.
- **Electric Vitality:** High-contrast neon accents against pitch-dark deep forest canvases channel adrenaline, speed, and court energy.
- **Curated Prestige:** Subtle warm gold accents for honors and ratings elevate the space from a neighborhood gym to a benchmark athletic sanctuary.

### Style Directives
- **Visual Movement:** Ultra-clean Modern Dark Mode reinforced with calibrated glassmorphic badges, micro-surface borders, and high-contrast typographic anchors.
- **Imagery Art Direction:** Rich atmospheric photography featuring high-speed athletic motion, warm court-timber textures, and cool ambient arena lighting.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast luminescence over a dark, deep-forest background foundation.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary (`#00e599` - Hyper Court Mint):** High-energy electric accent designated strictly for key interactive elements, primary conversions, booking highlights, and active states.
- **Secondary (`#ffb800` - Trophy Gold):** Reserved for star ratings, championship achievements, accolades, and premium feature badges.
- **Tertiary (`#38ef7d` - Emerald Pulse):** Supplemental gradient stops, success states, and subtle status rings.
- **Neutral Base (`#061510` & `#0b1f17` - Abyssal Forest):** The deep ambient dark foundation that delivers superior contrast without resorting to sterile pure black.

### Surface Color Logic
- **Canvas Base:** `#061510`
- **Surface Level 1 (Section Panels & Containers):** `#0b1f17`
- **Surface Level 2 (Cards & Modules):** `#112820`
- **Surface Level 3 (Inputs, Chips & Secondary Hovers):** `#18362b`
- **Borders & Dividers:** `rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.12)` for standard frames, escalating to `rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.35)` on hover.

## Typography

The type system utilizes Plus Jakarta Sans across all roles to ensure geometric balance, crisp legibility at glance speed, and refined athletic authority.

### Typographic Principles
- **Eyebrow Category Markers:** Rendered using `label-caps` in uppercase, paired with 60% opacity or primary mint color to establish clear section hierarchy.
- **High-Velocity Contrast:** Headlines utilize extra-bold and bold weights paired with tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic sports editorial impact.
- **Clarity in Dense Info:** Slot times, facility metrics, and location metadata strictly apply medium and semibold weights to preserve scan speed in low-light environments.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid system anchors the single-page layout, shifting seamlessly across viewport tiers.

### Breakpoints & Container Model
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, max container width of 1280px, centered with `margin-lg` outer canvas padding and `gutter-lg` column separations.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `margin-md` canvas padding, `gutter` column separations.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, full fluid width with `margin` safe margins and `gutter-sm` gaps.

### Spacing Cadence
- **Section Block Spacing:** 80px to 112px between major page movements to provide visual breathing room.
- **Internal Card Padding:** `space-lg` (24px) standard for cards; `space-md` (16px) for compact utility modules.
- **Micro Gaps:** `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px) for icon-label bindings and slot selector matrices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal surfaces and targeted luminous edge lighting rather than standard murky drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#061510` foundational depth.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Sub-sections):** `#0b1f17` elevated with crisp, hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Level 2 (Cards & Grid Units):** `#112820` backed by an ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Floating Elements, Dropdowns, Tooltips):** Frosted glassmorphism using `background: rgba(17, 40, 32, 0.85)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, and `border: 1px solid rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.25)`.
- **Primary Focus Glow:** Active booking chips and hero CTAs cast a signature mint glow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design adopts a refined 0.5rem (8px) base radius, delivering modern engineered precision that avoids overly aggressive blockiness while maintaining athletic discipline.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Action Buttons & Chips:** 8px (`rounded-md`) for structured items; select micro-pills use full radius (`rounded-full`) solely for floating status chips and category badges.
- **Cards & Content Frames:** 12px to 16px (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) for media modules and interactive amenity showcases.
- **Slot Selection Tiles:** 8px (`rounded-md`) for high rhythmic consistency inside the court scheduling grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book a Slot / Check Availability):** Background `#00e599`, text `#061510`, font-weight 700. Padding: 12px 24px. Micro-interaction: subtle scale up (`1.02`) on hover with shadow bloom `0 10px 24px rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary Action (Directions / Learn More):** Transparent background, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`, text `#ffffff`. Hover converts border to `rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.5)` with background `rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.06)`.
- **Icon Buttons:** Centered 44x44px target area with subtle circular border frame.

### Chips & Badges
- **Glass Status Pill:** Translucent background (`rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.1)`), mint border (`rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.3)`), text `#00e599`. Used for non-marking shoe notices and operational states.
- **Rating Counter:** Dark pill enclosure with `#ffb800` star iconography and bold contrast typography (`4.4 / 5`).

### Booking & Time Slot Grid
- **Default Slot:** `#112820` surface with border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Text `#ffffff`.
- **Selected Slot:** Solid `#00e599` surface, text `#061510`, font-weight 700.
- **Booked / Disabled Slot:** Opacity 0.35, strikethrough styling, pointer-events none.

### Cards & Media Panels
- **Facility Amenity Cards:** Top-weighted photography with dark forest body frame below. Clean separation using 1px border `rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.1)`.
- **Testimonial Modules:** Left quote mark accent in tertiary mint with reviewer verification stamp, high-contrast user avatars, and gold review stars.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Dark recessed background (`#081a13`), inset border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, padding 12px 16px.
- Focus state activates crisp mint ring `0 0 0 2px rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.4)`.

### Accordion (FAQ)
- Border-bottom separated or contained dark cards. Crisp `+` / `-` toggle morphs using primary mint rotation transition.
