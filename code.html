FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a slot?

You can book directly through our online slot booking selector above or through the Playo application. Just pick your preferred date and time, confirm, and pay at the venue or online.

What are the opening hours?

We are open 7 days a week from 6:00 AM in the morning to 12:00 AM midnight.

What facilities are available?

We provide two wooden badminton courts, a spacious viewing gallery, dedicated change rooms, showers, and separate clean washrooms.

Is there a dress code or footwear requirement?

Yes! Non-marking badminton shoes are mandatory to protect the polished wooden court surface. Regular running shoes or black soles are strictly not permitted on court.
